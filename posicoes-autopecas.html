Part position for every compatible vehicle

Sellers add the position of the part in the listing form, so buyers know if it fits front, rear, left or right.

Product and Content DesignerMercado Libre

Context

In auto parts listings, missing or duplicated information about where the part goes left buyers unsure, and turned into questions, complaints and returns for incompatibility. Sellers split this information between the technical sheet (61%) and the listing form (3%), which caused confusion and rework. The goal was to cut position complaints and grow auto parts sales in Argentina, Brazil, Mexico and Colombia.

Results

Handoff for 4 countries, in phases: new listings, existing listings and domains without positions.

One position field per vehicle, with no duplication between technical sheet and listing form.

Buyers see the right position in the compatibility widget, based on their own vehicle.

What I did

Ran the usability and content review, the user journey and the benchmark that defined the problems.

Explored the modal in UI and content rounds, up to high fidelity, and adapted the other impacts: compatibilities card, onboarding, top card, copy and paste and technical sheet.

Documented use cases, specs, singular and plural, limits and error states, and built the prototype for handoff.

What changed

Position became a field of the listing form, applied to all compatible vehicles at once, and left the technical sheet.

The modal shows the positions as checkboxes, instead of a long dropdown with wrong options.

When the right position is missing, the seller creates one by combining up to 4 areas of the vehicle.

Clear errors when a position was already created or already selected.
