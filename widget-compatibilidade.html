A compatibility widget that helps people buy the right part

Each widget state has one goal: buy knowing it fits, find a compatible option or check with the seller.

Product and Content DesignerMercado Libre

Context

In research, 69% of searches didn’t show the widget, 24% of people didn’t remember seeing it and 41% found inconsistent information. Buyers said they saw no value in the widget, and incompatibility complaints were high, 9% of them because of position. UX goal: people use the widget and buy the right part for their vehicle. Business goal: fewer incompatibility complaints.

Results

Handoff for 6 countries, in the app and on desktop.

4 states with a defined goal and copy, from empty to unknown.

Position and notes next to the vehicle, right where the buying decision happens.

What I did

Defined a goal and the copy for each widget state: empty, compatible, incompatible and unknown.

Ran a design critique with two proposals and used the feedback, like the visual difference between states and the weight of the exit action, to refine the final version.

Organized the handoff with structure, behaviors, specs, accessibility and a native prototype of the happy path.

What changed

Compatible: green header, the vehicle, and the positions and notes that apply, so the person buys knowing it fits.

Incompatible or unknown: the widget points to “See compatible products” when there is an offer, and encourages checking with the seller when there isn’t.

Empty: invites people to add their vehicle, to raise widget usage.

Same structure in the app and on desktop.
