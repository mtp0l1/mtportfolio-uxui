A compatibilities card that shows sellers the next step

The text and the main button change with what the listing already has, in the create and edit flows.

Product and Content DesignerMercado Libre

Context

In the listing form, the compatibilities card looked the same for every listing, with or without compatible vehicles. Sellers didn’t know what to do next, and listings without compatibilities get more questions and incompatibility complaints. The goal was to adjust the wording and the structure of the card for each use case, in 6 countries.

Results

4 use cases, each with its own text and main button.

The same logic in the create and edit flows.

Handoff for 6 countries: Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, Uruguay, Colombia and Chile.

What I did

Mapped the use cases in the create and edit flows and defined the main and secondary button for each one.

Wrote the card copy for each state, with the benefit of adding vehicles and the risk of complaints.

Documented the structure, definitions, use cases and specs for handoff.

What changed

No compatibilities: “Add compatible vehicles” is the main action, with the benefit up front.

With compatibilities: the card sums up what was added, vehicles, notes and positions, and puts “Edit added vehicles” first.

With suggestions: “Review suggested vehicles” leads, explaining they are highly likely to fit.

With an exception: the seller is invited to review the vehicle catalog.
