Generic AI images to list auto parts without photos

A JSON prompt with validation rules creates standard reference images, so sellers can list parts that had no photo.

Product and Content DesignerMercado Libre

Context

12% of auto parts products, about 1.7 million, had no images and couldn’t be created in the catalog, so they couldn’t be listed. Sellers couldn’t sell parts they already had, and buyers found less to choose from. The goal was to grow the auto parts offer by creating generic images with AI.

Results

98% approval rate in the catalog with the JSON method.

500+ product domains and about 1.8 million products ready to be listed, in all countries.

Conversion and product detail metrics are still being built and will be tracked after the full rollout.

What I did

Tested generation from reference photos in ImageFX, Veo3, GPT Image and DALL·E 3. The first version failed: images weren’t faithful, were hard to control and the best tool wasn’t approved.

Moved to a JSON prompt in blocks, instruction, core, custom and validation, with rules against off-white backgrounds and wrong angles and an instruction to regenerate when a rule breaks.

Validated with Product, Business and IT the 3D illustration style, over realistic and schematic, and the switch from reference image to domain name.

Designed the “Reference photo” disclaimer for the listing flow, search and product page.

What changed

Products without photos get a 3D illustration on a white background, in the listing flow, in search and on the product page.

The “Reference photo” label sets expectations: the image represents the part, it isn’t the real product.

The prompt only needs the product domain name, so IT could turn it into a bot.
